\section{Results}

The synthesis of the $120$ included studies highlights the operational landscape of context-aware digital mental health interventions through five primary research queries: [cite: 12]
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Which specific mental health conditions have been prioritized by context-aware interventions over time, and what hardware platforms are deployed to target them?
    \item What specific data collection mechanisms are utilized to capture context, and is the field succeeding in reducing user burden through passive sensing?
    \item How evenly balanced is the tracking of user context across physical, social, and computing domains, and what does this reveal about algorithmic intervention triggers?
    \item What parameters are researchers actively evaluating when validating these context-aware platforms, and does the focus lie on technical execution or therapeutic value?
    \item How are psychological theories translated into these context-aware platforms, and does the methodological design of the studies support clinical efficacy or merely technical feasibility?
\end{enumerate}
